My Engineering Origin Story

My fascination with Computer Engineering began at age thirteen when I took apart a broken desktop motherboard to see how the CPU communicated with memory. Seeing copper traces route signals at billions of cycles per second convinced me that the most exciting work happens right at the boundary between physical hardware and software.

Over the past four years, I have progressed from blinking my first LED on a breadboard to designing multi-layer PCBs, writing real-time firmware in C++, and synthesizing soft-core processors in Verilog. As I enter university for my B.S. in Computer Engineering, I aim to join an embedded systems or computer architecture research lab and contribute to energy-efficient computing.
